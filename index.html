Decision rule

Price alone does not break a thesis.

Normal volatility is expected. A status should worsen only when fundamental evidence, competitive position, or the underlying reason for owning an asset materially deteriorates.

  • 🟢 HOLD — thesis intact.
  • 🟡 MONITOR — meaningful warning, not a sell signal.
  • 🟠 REVIEW — multiple independent warning signals.
  • 🔴 THESIS AT RISK — core ownership reason materially broken.
